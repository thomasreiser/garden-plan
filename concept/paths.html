Whimsical garden: path concept (draft)

Top view, north up, street on the right. Grid = 1 m. Existing paving, terrace, play area, raised beds and stepping stones stay as they are.

Lighting plan

  1. ┅String lightsSpanned across the garden: from a post at the raised beds to the terrace roof, and from the roof on to a post in the play-area corner. Also along the arch and around the pavilion's eaves.
  2. ■LanternsA few hanging in the serviceberry over the seating nook; a pendant in the pavilion.
  3. ●Low path lightsKnee-high mushroom lights along the forest path and at the checkerboard path (none on the footpath outside the garden).
  4. ▲UplightsWillow, Japanese maple, peach, globe locust (from below), the standing stone, and a few spots grazing the bamboo screens from the front.
  5. ━LED strips (existing)Set into the lawn along the north and south house paths, from their west end 10 m toward the street; and around the top border of the existing raised bed.
  6. ◖Path bollardsLow louvred bollards about every 3 m along the glow path, alternating sides, throwing soft indirect light onto the stones only.
  7. ◗Wall lightsAbove the shed doors. On the house only the existing lights by the front door and the terrace door; no other lights on the house walls.
  8. ✦Fairy ring and greenhouseSmall mushroom lights in the fairy ring; warm string light inside the greenhouse.

Warm white (≤ 2700 K) throughout, low and shielded. The open west lawn, the play area and the raised beds stay dark. Garden lights on a timer that switches off late at night (insects, neighbours).

Paths

  1. Existing stepping stones (kept)Terrace corner to the raised beds, with the fork to the planned bed, and terrace corner to the play area.
  2. Little forest path (north lawn)Mossy flagstones with thyme in the joints. Starts at the garden shed's doors, winds west across the north lawn and joins the existing stepping stones. A short spur under the arch joins the house's north path.
  3. Glow path (south lawn)Winding light flagstones through the middle of the south lawn, lit indirectly by low bollards every few metres, with a planting bed between it and the house path. Runs from the play area along the south lawn, past the open front of the pavilion, to the garden door at the house corner by the heat pump, with a link to the house's south path.

Features along the way

  1. Fairy-light archWhere the spur leaves the house's north path into the north lawn.
  2. Cottage borderFoxgloves, alliums, lavender along the north fence. The fence is the neighbour's double-rod mesh and stays open: only a few light climbers (clematis) with gaps between.
  3. Low border along the houseFerns and hostas in the shade, from the arch to the garden door.
  4. Mosaic seating nookSmall checkerboard patch of pink tiles and thyme, with a mosaic table and lanterns, in the bend of the forest path.
  5. Kitchen cornerExisting and planned raised bed, where path A forks.
  6. Terrace6 × 3 m roof against the house toward the north end (living-room door still under it), with downlights; long table with lanterns under its north half.
  7. Peach tree (moved)Existing young tree, replanted on the south lawn west of the pavilion, in front of the bamboo.
  8. Stone cairnsSmall gravel bed of cairns.
  9. Weeping willow, ~4.5 m high, ~2 m wideJust south of the glow path near the garden door: a slender curtain, with stone cairns at its foot.
  10. Bamboo and tall-plant screenAlong the whole south fence, along the fence directly at the street (blocking the view in), plus one clump in the play area by the west wall: clumping bamboo (with root barrier), Miscanthus, Joe-Pye weed and hollyhocks, with lower flowers in front.
  11. Garden door (south)At the house corner, from the footpath to the south lawn.
  12. Garden shed (planned)4.00 × 2.14 m, anthracite, west of the future carport, close to the house path and 1 m off the neighbour fence, doors facing the garden.
  13. Garden door (north)Across the house path between the shed and the house, plus a short fence behind the shed, separating the driveway from the garden.
  14. Globe locustCentred across the front lawn, toward its south (footpath) end, with a ring of catmint below. Low lavender band along the street edge.
  15. Front facadeTwo narrow beds of low perennials either side of the door, two large pots by the entrance.
  16. Wooden pavilion, 3 × 3 mOn the south lawn in front of the bamboo screen (not against the fence), open toward the house, reached from the glow path.
  17. Arch plantingBed west of the arch with a red Japanese maple, hydrangeas and ferns; low border continues east of it. Climbing rose on the arch.
  18. Serviceberry (multi-stem)Small tree shading the seating nook; white spring blossom, red autumn colour.
  19. Pavilion plantingHydrangeas and tall grasses on both sides of the pavilion.
  20. Mythical garden room: round courseThe small lawn by the street, enclosed by bamboo. A round path of pink tiles and thyme in a checkerboard loops around a fairy ring of mushroom lights with a mossy standing stone in the middle; twisted hazel, ferns and foxgloves around it.
  21. Small greenhouse, ~2.0 × 2.5 mGlass greenhouse against the street-side bamboo, door opening onto the round path.
  22. Small checkerboard patchPink tiles and thyme, from the garden door (where the glow path ends) into the round course, which uses the same checkerboard.
  23. Snacking hedge (Naschhecke)Red, white and black currants, gooseberries and jostaberry in front of the south bamboo: between willow and garden door, beside the peach tree, and near the play area.
  24. Berry trellisRaspberries and thornless blackberries on a wire trellis along the footpath fence inside the mythical room; gooseberries along its south edge; wild strawberries as ground cover inside the fairy ring.
  25. Elderberry (Holunder)Large native shrub in the south-east corner beside the greenhouse; flowers for syrup, berries in autumn.
